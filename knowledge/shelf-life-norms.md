# Shelf-Life Norms Reference

## Purpose

This file is the authoritative reference the **What the Fridge** app agent uses to estimate freshness windows for ingredients. When a user adds an item to their inventory without a specific expiration date, the agent consults these norms to assign a default "use-by" window and to prioritize ingredients in meal-plan suggestions.

## Scope

These are **general guidelines for quality and typical safety**, not food-safety guarantees. Actual shelf life varies with packaging, handling, initial freshness at purchase, and storage conditions. When in doubt, users should follow the printed date on the package or consult a food-safety authority.

## Important Notes

1. **"Past printed date"** means past the best-by or sell-by date on the package. The food is often still safe to consume, but quality (taste, texture, nutritional value) may decline.
2. **Freezer times are for quality, not safety.** Properly frozen food held at 0 °F (−18 °C) is safe indefinitely; the times listed indicate when quality begins to degrade.
3. **These norms assume proper storage** at recommended temperatures: refrigerator at or below 40 °F (4 °C) and freezer at 0 °F (−18 °C).
4. **Once a sealed package is opened**, the shelf life resets to the "opened" window listed in these tables.
5. **The agent uses these norms as defaults.** Users can always override with a specific expiry date entered manually; the manual date takes precedence.
